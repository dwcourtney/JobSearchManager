## Human adjudication rubric

**Question:** Does the posting describe substantive work within a broad technical, engineering or plausibly adjacent occupational universe that should continue downstream?

Use **KEEP / REJECT / AMBIGUOUS**, with high/medium/low confidence, a short reason citing duties, reviewer and UTC date. AMBIGUOUS means retain for downstream assessment; it is distinct from runtime UNDETERMINED caused by a timeout. Leave unreviewed rows blank. Do not prefill a human decision from the machine audit classification.

1. Read duty excerpts before the prior audit opinion. If decisive context is missing, request the archived full description or choose AMBIGUOUS. The archive reference is supplied; no fresh posting fetch is necessary.
2. **KEEP** when substantive technical/engineering design, analysis, implementation, diagnosis, configuration, testing, integration or technical leadership is part of the job. It need not be software work, the majority of every minute, or personally attractive. Mixed duties can qualify.
3. **REJECT** only when primary work is clearly unrelated and there is no substantive technical/engineering component. Merely using software, selling technology, recruiting engineers or working for a technical employer is insufficient for KEEP. Routine tools and procedural checks are not automatically engineering analysis.
4. **AMBIGUOUS** when duties cannot resolve a genuine boundary or the description mixes responsibilities without enough detail. Prefer continuation over guessing; do not label low-confidence REJECT to increase volume.
5. Ignore pay, location, relocation, Antarctica/field deployment, citizenship/clearance, degree/licensure, seniority, workflow state and personal Job Fit score when deciding occupational scope. Those can matter downstream. Qualifications can corroborate a duty but cannot substitute for it.

Specific boundaries:

- **Technicians:** calibration, instrument diagnostics, fault isolation and engineering test interpretation may qualify; a technician title is neither proof of relevance nor a rejection reason. Routine parts replacement/cleaning/loading by checklist alone can still be unrelated. Record the actual diagnostic/analysis task.
- **Application support:** distinguish correcting integrations/configuration/software faults from correcting payroll transactions or answering ordinary process questions. HR/finance enterprise support remains technical despite its business domain.
- **Civil/structural/power:** genuine design, calculations, simulation, engineering drawings and technical review should KEEP under the clarified scope. “Civil” and “construction” cannot alone exclude an engineering occupation. Nontechnical construction labor/administration remains distinguishable by duties. CAD use is supporting context, not automatic engineering proof.
- **Electronics/test/lab:** PCB layouts, constraints, schematic implementation, test-system design and analytical instrument support belong in scope. A production testing/inspection position can remain ambiguous if it gives only procedural pass/fail work.
- **Project/leadership:** assess technical accountability, not title prestige. Engineering design authority, architecture and system-integration leadership qualify; schedule/budget/commercial ownership alone does not. Hands-on coding is not required for a genuine technical lead.
- **Field roles:** setting is not exclusion. SATCOM/COMSEC system support, controls/electronic maintenance and instrumentation can qualify. Shipping COMSEC equipment, guarding a facility or operating vehicles is not the same occupation.

For mixed roles, ask: **What technical decision, deliverable or diagnosis is this employee responsible for?** If the description supports one, retain; if it describes only a technology product/customer or generic “systems/development,” do not infer one. Record unresolved disagreement instead of changing scope to fit a metric.

Review the five LIKELY WRONG cases (Q10, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q26) first for urgency, but consider the civil group under the newly clarified scope rather than inheriting its old “personal search space” uncertainty. Ideally a second reviewer checks disagreements/high-impact boundaries. The 29 selected cases are development/safety sentinels after review, not an independent holdout.
